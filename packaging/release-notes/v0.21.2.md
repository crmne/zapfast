On a Mac, `brew install --cask crmne/tap/zapfast`. On Arch, `yay -S zapfast-bin`. On Fedora, `sudo dnf copr enable paolino/zapfast` and then `sudo dnf install zapfast`, which keeps ZapFast updated with the rest of your system.

## New

- **A person's details list the groups you share with them.** Under the media in their info panel, each group you are both in, by name; click one to open it. Locked groups stay in the locked folder. By @Goodly58. (#786)
- **ZapFast speaks Dutch.** The whole interface, following your system's language. By @TheDutchSmoke. (#770)
- **Search finds people by the start of any word in their name.** In the chat search (Ctrl+K), `will shak` finds William Shakespeare and `j d` finds John Doe, as New chat already did. By @Mayank-Bhatasana. (#775)
- **New chat fits its list to your search, and Enter opens the first match.** The first matching contact is highlighted while you type, and the list shrinks to the matches instead of leaving an empty space. By @Mayank-Bhatasana. (#777)

## Fixed

- **On macOS, the traffic lights have a strip of their own when the chat list is hidden.** They used to crowd the Chats button and hang past the edge of the narrow rail onto the chat header. Now a slim strip in the rail's colour runs across the top of the window and holds only them. By @crmne; thanks @guidofraga, who reported it. (#857)
- **On macOS, closing the window keeps ZapFast running even without its menu-bar item.** Turning the item off made closing the window quit ZapFast, so notifications stopped. Now the Dock brings the window back and Command-Q quits, as with other Mac apps; the setting, shown as "Show in the menu bar", decides only the item. By @felipefdl. (#776)
- **The info panel's header lines up with the chat header with every font.** 0.21.1 fixed it when the chat header was the taller of the two; with some fonts the info panel's was, and its edge still sat a few points lower. By @crmne. (#639)

## Known limitations

- Calls are still a work in progress, as described in [0.20.0](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/releases/tag/v0.20.0): video calls are in alpha, and in group calls your video stops for a moment every few seconds (#592).
